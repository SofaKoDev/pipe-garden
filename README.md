# Pipe Garden

A cozy, kid-friendly Pipe Dream puzzle game where you tap pipes to rotate them and guide sparkling water from the brass garden faucet to the thirsty flowers and happy frog pond!

- **Live site:** https://sofakodev.github.io/pipe-garden/
- **Difficulty Sizes:**
  - **Sprout (4×4):** Gentle starter garden for little hands
  - **Bloom (5×5):** Medium garden with playful twists
  - **Meadow (6×6):** Bigger winding garden puzzle
  - **Orchard (7×7):** Master gardener challenge
- **Modes:**
  - **Relaxed (default):** Rotate pipes at your own pace, then tap **Turn On Water!**
  - **Timed:** Classic Pipe Dream countdown — connect the pipes before the water rushes out!
- **Controls:** Tap or click any pipe tile to rotate it 90°.
